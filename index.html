Questions

Can you see my chats?

No. Threadstash has no server. Your chats are saved in your own browser, and uninstalling the extension deletes them.

How do I unlock Pro?

Buy a plan from the extension. You get a license key by email. Paste it into Threadstash and Pro turns on.

Does it change how ChatGPT or Claude look?

No. Threadstash only reads the pages. Everything it shows is in Chrome's side panel, next to the site.
