What it does

Folders

Group chats from ChatGPT and Claude together, by project, client or topic.

Search

Find a chat by title, or search the full text of every conversation with Pro.

Export

Save chats as Markdown files you own, ready for notes or backups.

Private by design

Chats are stored only in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking.
