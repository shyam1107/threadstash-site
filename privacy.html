Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Threadstash is a Chrome extension that stores your ChatGPT and Claude chat titles and contents only in your browser. We do not run a server that receives your chats, and we do not collect personal data through the extension.

What the extension stores

Chat titles, links and text from chatgpt.com and claude.ai, and the folders you create. This data is kept in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the extension can access

The extension reads pages on chatgpt.com and claude.ai to save your chats. To check a Pro license, it sends your license key to our payment provider, Dodo Payments. It accesses no other sites.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record. Dodo Payments receives the details you enter at checkout, such as your email address and payment information, and handles them under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.

Analytics and tracking

None. The extension and this website use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

"Remove from Threadstash" deletes a chat from the extension. Uninstalling the extension deletes all data it stored.
